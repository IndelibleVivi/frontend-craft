# Style slots: practiced, repeatable design units

Use when a researched and practiced solution can make the current work better,
or a useful completed result should become reusable. A slot preserves a known
way to obtain an effect, with its actual materials and implementation. Fixed
choices are useful when they preserve a successful relationship. Reuse does
not require inventing a different design on every task.

A slot can cover a whole experience, a visual language, a local composition and
interaction, or one precise making technique. These are useful scales, not a
required hierarchy. A whole may be kept intact; a local mechanism need not
carry its source product's entire skin. Do not create an application theme
engine, registry service, or schema migration just to store these materials.

## What makes a slot usable

Keep the smallest record that lets another maker reproduce the result and
understand its limits, beside the existing example or project design source:

- **Effect and conditions:** the wanted result, content, language, platform,
  viewport, and use state where it worked; what part of the whole it serves.
- **Actual instance:** a viewable artifact or replay path, canonical source,
  required assets/fonts and their provenance. A style adjective is insufficient.
- **Fixed relationships and allowed changes:** what must survive, what may vary,
  and which neighboring regions or states would need to change with it.
- **Evidence and limits:** what was researched, implemented, rendered, exercised,
  and accepted by whom at what scope; one counterexample or failure condition
  that would change the choice.

Untried references are research candidates. A practiced mechanism can have
useful evidence while its visual treatment remains provisional. State that
scope plainly rather than awarding a universal maturity score. Personal
acceptance is separate from agent craft judgment and technical behavior.
Do not turn a rejected composition into a successful visual slot by retaining
only its passing interaction tests.

## Select and compose through the whole

Start from this task's purpose, actual content, relevant scoped preferences,
and provisional whole. Choose a slot because it can carry a needed relationship,
not merely because a keyword matched. Look at its actual instance before using
it. Make ordinary reversible choices without asking the user to fill slots.

Preserve its fixed core and adapt the permitted variables. If a new language,
asset, content volume, state model, or viewport violates the demonstrated
conditions, use the mechanism as a starting hypothesis and verify the changed
part. Do not claim inherited proof for the new conditions.

Check combinations in context: a bold navigation, expressive heading, and rich
card can each work alone while competing in one screen. Choose what leads,
subordinate the rest, and follow the real use sequence. A composition that works
on entry may need a different balance while editing or returning. [Build](build.md)
owns integration; [QA](qa-contract.md) owns the affected checks.

## Current local entries

### Visible choice with a named live result

- **Scale/role:** local composition and interaction; connects comparison to
  consequence inside a larger selection experience.
- **Instance:** [choice-and-result study](../examples/showcase/studies/choice-and-result/index.html),
  [implementation](../examples/showcase/studies/choice-and-result/app.js),
  [layout](../examples/showcase/studies/choice-and-result/styles.css).
- **Fixed:** one selected record drives every output; language changes translate
  that identity. The named result and its description remain reachable from
  the working choices.
  Focus, selected state, and presentation mode retain separate meaning.
- **Variable:** content, illustration, type/color treatment, and option count.
  Switching from exposed options to a menu trades comparison for compactness;
  the current result must still be identifiable.
- **Conditions/limits:** exclusive finite choices, authored bilingual records,
  short/long copy and 3/12 options. Reload resets this study. It does not prove
  saving, search of huge collections, or exact numeric entry. A different
  domain should retain semantics rather than pretend these controls cover it.
- **Evidence status:** an authored working mechanism and inspectable artwork;
  visual treatment is a candidate, not an owner-approved style or autonomous
  first-draft quality benchmark. See the [study guide](../examples/showcase/README.md#making-study-choice-and-result)
  for the current reproduction path and claims.

### Iridescent folded sculpture

- **Scale/role:** a making technique and material treatment for an expressive
  code-rendered work; the current composition places the sculpture at the center.
- **Instance:** [Liquid Matter](../examples/showcase/studies/choice-and-result/index.html),
  [renderer](../examples/showcase/studies/choice-and-result/sculpture.js), and
  [source guide](../examples/showcase/studies/choice-and-result/README.md).
- **Fixed:** inspectable folded silhouette, broad studio reflection plus a
  narrower highlight, a coherent optical color phase, grazing-edge response,
  and a background against which the shape remains legible. The considered
  paused pose must work without continuous motion.
- **Variable:** ring modulation, cross-section, color phase, turn, background,
  type placement, and scene size. Changing geometry or camera can require
  retuning reflections and the whole composition; replacing only hue is not
  a new visual language.
- **Conditions/limits:** WebGL, one bounded procedural sculpture, no texture
  or model assets. Current evidence is Chromium at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844
  with both languages. It does not establish mobile GPU performance, physical
  optical accuracy, an arbitrary model renderer, or an accepted personal style.
- **Evidence status:** implemented and rendered; actual pixels responded to
  keyboard rotation and time, stopped changing after pause, and the narrow
  composition was revised for long text. This is a guided candidate technique;
  owner aesthetic acceptance remains unverified.

The [making recipes](reference-sources.md) provide source-grounded candidates
at other scales. Their existence is not evidence that each has been practiced
locally. Add an actual whole-experience or visual-language slot when its work
and evidence exist; do not fill a catalog with invented successes.

## Learn without flattening the work

Retain a successful whole with its materials and use conditions, then identify
which relationships transfer. A whole-result acceptance does not prove approval
of every incidental font or icon. A rejected transfer may narrow the slot's
conditions without invalidating its original result.

Keep public, independently authored examples in the distributable package.
Personal artifacts and scoped feedback stay in the authorized private case
store; link through [design learning](design-learning.md) rather than copying
that history into a slot catalog. On a later task, observe which making choice
the slot changed and what happened. Stable reuse should reduce repeated basic
instruction while leaving new aims and explicit style changes open.
